Adzan TV: background ornaments

The Kiswah screensaver with each candidate ornament behind it. A is what the TV shows now. B, C and D are traditional geometric patterns built the way craftsmen draw them (Hankin's "polygons in contact" method on a real tiling), and they fade out behind the clock and prayer times so the text stays clean. E keeps the wallpaper quiet and puts the ornament in a frieze band instead.
